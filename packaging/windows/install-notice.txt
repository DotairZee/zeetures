Zeetures is a noncommercial fan fork of openc2e, the open-source engine for
the Creatures games, focused on Creatures 2.

You need your own copy of Creatures 2
-------------------------------------
Zeetures contains no part of the game: no sprites, sounds, genomes,
scripts or worlds. It plays from your own installed copy: Creatures 2
comes with "Creatures: The Albian Years" on Steam, and is also sold on
GOG. Zeetures looks for it in the usual Steam and GOG folders. If it
can't find it, it asks you for the folder the first time it starts, and
remembers it.

Creatures, Norns, Grendels, Ettins and Albia belong to Gameware Europe.
This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Gameware.

Your worlds and saves are kept in Documents\My Games\Creatures 2, never in
the game's own folder or here. Uninstalling Zeetures leaves them there.
