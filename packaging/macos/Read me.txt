ZEETURES FOR MAC
================

Zeetures is a noncommercial fan fork of openc2e, the open-source engine
for the Creatures games, focused on Creatures 2. It runs on Apple Silicon
and Intel Macs, macOS 10.15 or later.

1. Drag Zeetures into Applications.

2. The first time, open it from Applications. macOS says it can't check
   it for malicious software (Zeetures is not signed by Apple). Click
   Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
   "Zeetures was blocked", click Open Anyway, and confirm. (On macOS 14
   and earlier: right-click Zeetures, choose Open, then Open.) After that
   it opens normally.

3. Zeetures needs your own copy of Creatures 2 and asks for its folder
   the first time. Steam for Mac won't install the game (it is listed as
   Windows-only), but it downloads the files. The steps:
   https://github.com/DotairZee/zeetures#on-a-mac
   If you copy them to ~/Games/Creatures The Albian Years, Zeetures finds
   them on its own.

Zeetures contains no part of the game: no sprites, sounds, genomes,
scripts or worlds. Creatures, Norns, Grendels, Ettins and Albia belong to
Gameware Europe. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Gameware.

Your worlds and saves are kept in Documents/openc2e Data/Creatures 2.
