# Scents (pheromones) for openc2e's Creatures 2 worlds (our own design, owner
# 2026-11-11). Installed as ~/.openc2e/Creatures 2/pheromones.txt; read again
# at each world load. Remove the file and there are no scents at all.
#
# Each room holds every scent named below. Every 5 world ticks a scent fades
# a little, flows through open doorways toward rooms with less of it, and is
# topped up by whoever is giving it off there. Creatures smell the room they
# are in and, for following, the rooms beyond its doorways. A creature does
# not smell a scent it is giving off itself (it is used to its own odor).
#
# ---- Lines ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#
#   scent NAME [spread S] [fade F] [habituate T]
#       S: share of the difference that flows through a fully open doorway
#          each step (0-0.24, default 0.12); F: share lost each step (default
#          0.02; 0.02 is about a minute to fade by half at normal speed).
#       T: habituation: a creature in the scent gets used to it over about
#          T ticks (10 a second), and everything it does for the scent
#          (follow, avoid, chem) weakens by as much; away from it, it
#          unlearns at the same pace. Default none.
#
#   WHO emit SCENT STRENGTH [when CONDITIONS]
#       gives off the scent (1 = a typical creature's worth).
#   object FAMILY GENUS SPECIES emit SCENT STRENGTH
#       objects too (0 means any), e.g. eggs, a nest, a food bowl.
#   WHO smell SCENT chem CHEMICAL AMOUNT [when CONDITIONS]
#       "primer": smelling it adds the chemical, AMOUNT a step at full
#       strength (C2 chemical numbers, as in the Science Kit).
#   WHO smell SCENT follow [for DRIVE] [WEIGHT] [tend] [when CONDITIONS]
#   WHO smell SCENT avoid [for DRIVE] [WEIGHT] [when CONDITIONS]
#       "releaser": an urge to go where it is stronger, or away from it,
#       weighed against the creature's other needs. "for DRIVE": worth as
#       much as easing that drive (pain pleasure hunger cold hot tired sleepy
#       lonely crowded fear bored anger sex injury suffocate thirst stress);
#       without it, a fixed urge (WEIGHT 1 is a middling one); a tend urge
#       without a drive fades as the creature's most pressing need (hunger,
#       pain, cold, heat, tiredness, sleepiness, fear, injury, air, thirst)
#       grows. "tend": with
#       one of its own kind who needs care (a baby, a child, the senile, the ill) in
#       sight, the creature goes to it, tickles it, and brings it food or a
#       toy (picks one up nearby, carries it over, drops it beside them).
#   WHO smell SCENT bring CATEGORY[,CATEGORY] [WEIGHT] [drop D] [when CONDITIONS]
#       carries things of those categories (C2's: 5 egg, 6 food, 9 gadget,
#       13 toy, 25 fruit ...) up the scent: picks up the nearest it can reach
#       where the scent is fainter than D (default 0.5) and a trail leads
#       on, and puts it down where the scent is at least D.
#   WHO mark harm|help|felt:N,N SCENT STRENGTH TICKS [when CONDITIONS]
#       when something harms (pain, injury, punishment) or helps (reward, a
#       drive eased) this creature, that thing is marked: it gives off the
#       scent wherever it goes, fading over TICKS (10 ticks a second).
#
#   WHO: any, norn, grendel (grendel.boney or grendel.bulbous for one kind)
#        or ettin; then :female or :male; then @ and a
#        life stage or range: baby child adolescent youth adult old senile
#        (or 0-6). Examples: norn, norn:female, any@baby, grendel:male@youth-old
#   CONDITIONS: ill (has an illness), CHEM>N, CHEM<N (body chemicals; a drive's own chemical is
#        its drive number plus one: 1 Pain ... 3 Hunger ... 8 Loneliness,
#        10 Fear, 13 Sex Drive), carrying, empty (carrying nothing), dead,
#        asleep, awake, day, night, wary (fell ill soon after being near a
#        grendel); joined with
#        "and". For smell rules also here>X and here<X: the scent's strength
#        where the creature is (0-1), for "dose bands": one scent, different
#        responses at different strengths.
#
# Watch it: OPENC2E_WATCH_SCENTS=1 prints the spread of each scent and every
# mark now and then.

# ---- Mates find each other by scent (on) ----------------------------------------
# Adolescents to old creatures give off their kind's and sex's scent. The
# other sex smelling it gets the genome's own mating chemistry going (the
# opposite-sex and species pheromones, 120 and 116, which react into the
# mating pheromone 115 and, with arousal, sex drive 13), and, once sex drive
# is up, follows the scent to its source.

scent norn-female
scent norn-male
scent grendel-female
scent grendel-male
scent ettin-female
scent ettin-male

norn:female@adolescent-old emit norn-female 1
norn:male@adolescent-old emit norn-male 1
grendel:female@adolescent-old emit grendel-female 1
grendel:male@adolescent-old emit grendel-male 1
ettin:female@adolescent-old emit ettin-female 1
ettin:male@adolescent-old emit ettin-male 1

norn:male@adolescent-old smell norn-female chem 120 3
norn:male@adolescent-old smell norn-female chem 116 2
norn:male@adolescent-old smell norn-female follow for sex 3 when 13>48
norn:female@adolescent-old smell norn-male chem 120 3
norn:female@adolescent-old smell norn-male chem 116 2
norn:female@adolescent-old smell norn-male follow for sex 3 when 13>48

grendel:male@adolescent-old smell grendel-female chem 120 3
grendel:male@adolescent-old smell grendel-female chem 116 2
grendel:male@adolescent-old smell grendel-female follow for sex 3 when 13>48
grendel:female@adolescent-old smell grendel-male chem 120 3
grendel:female@adolescent-old smell grendel-male chem 116 2
grendel:female@adolescent-old smell grendel-male follow for sex 3 when 13>48

ettin:male@adolescent-old smell ettin-female chem 120 3
ettin:male@adolescent-old smell ettin-female chem 116 2
ettin:male@adolescent-old smell ettin-female follow for sex 3 when 13>48
ettin:female@adolescent-old smell ettin-male chem 120 3
ettin:female@adolescent-old smell ettin-male chem 116 2
ettin:female@adolescent-old smell ettin-male follow for sex 3 when 13>48

# ---- Care: the young and the senile are tended (on; owner, 2026-11-15) ---------
# Babies, children and the senile give off their kind's "care" scent. Grown
# creatures of the same kind follow it, a steady pull that fades as their own
# most pressing need grows (owner's choice, 2026-11-15; weighed as loneliness
# it never fired in a crowded world, where nobody is lonely), and on finding
# one tickle it and bring it food or a toy. The same one is tickled
# again only after about a minute and a half, brought something after three.

scent norn-care fade 0.04
scent grendel-care fade 0.04
scent ettin-care fade 0.04

norn@baby-child emit norn-care 1
norn@senile emit norn-care 1
grendel@baby-child emit grendel-care 1
grendel@senile emit grendel-care 1
ettin@baby-child emit ettin-care 1
ettin@senile emit ettin-care 1
# The ill give it off too, so others tend them (owner, 2026-11-21: "pheromones
# similar to those that babies give off ... when creatures are sick, so that
# other creatures care for them"); grendels carry germs but are not ill.
norn emit norn-care 1 when ill
ettin emit ettin-care 1 when ill
# And the poisoned (heavy metals, 140, over 50; owner, 2026-11-21): those
# caring for them bring what they have learned eases pain, the volcanic
# bushes' fruit once its use is known.
norn emit norn-care 1 when 140>50
ettin emit ettin-care 1 when 140>50

norn@adolescent-old smell norn-care follow 1 tend
grendel@adolescent-old smell grendel-care follow 1 tend
ettin@adolescent-old smell ettin-care follow 1 tend

# ---- Grendels and ettins (on; owner, 2026-11-26: "let's do it!") ---------------
# Each kind gives off a scent of its own, and the others answer to it (our own
# design, from what their genes already hold; Kinds.h in the engine). Their
# homes give off scents too: the grendels' lair (the egg pile's keeper,
# 2 1 52), the ettins' den (the egg machine by the volcano, 2 1 25) and the
# incubator (3 7 1); and each ettin's hoard, a quiet spot it found
# ("ettin-hoard", laid by the engine). "bring" carries things up a scent and
# puts them down where it is at least "drop".
# New words for these rules: grendel.boney / grendel.bulbous / grendel.grouchy
# (Boney grendels have working reproductive genes; C2's own, bulbous, do
# not; grouchy grendels, the C1 grendels, are the C1-in-C2 Grendel pack's,
# on the engine's own genome; "grendel.c1" works too); conditions day,
# night, empty (carrying nothing) and wary (a creature that fell ill soon
# after being near a grendel, and has learned to link the two).

scent norn-kind fade 0.03
scent grendel-kind fade 0.03
scent boney-kind fade 0.03
scent grouchy-kind fade 0.03
scent ettin-kind fade 0.03
scent grendel-lair spread 0.16 fade 0.01
scent ettin-den spread 0.16 fade 0.01
scent incubator spread 0.16 fade 0.01
scent ettin-hoard spread 0.14 fade 0.015
scent hungry fade 0.04
scent egg spread 0.1 fade 0.03
scent carrion fade 0.02

norn emit norn-kind 1
grendel.bulbous emit grendel-kind 1
grendel.boney emit grendel-kind 1
grendel.boney emit boney-kind 1
grendel.grouchy emit grouchy-kind 1
ettin emit ettin-kind 1
object 2 1 52 emit grendel-lair 3
object 2 1 25 emit ettin-den 3
object 3 7 1 emit incubator 3
object 2 5 0 emit egg 0.5
any emit hungry 1 when 3>150
any emit carrion 1 when dead

# (chemicals: 3 Hunger, 9 Crowded.)
# Norns that have fallen ill after being near a grendel keep away from them;
# the young learn it the same way. Ettins keep away from grendels.
norn smell grendel-kind avoid for fear 2 when wary
ettin smell grendel-kind avoid for fear 1.5

# Bulbous grendels: lie up at the lair by day, prowl by night after norns
# (their genes: boredom draws them to norns, anger makes them hit) and bully
# ettins; eggs they find go to the lair.
grendel.bulbous smell grendel-lair follow 1.5 when day and awake and empty
grendel.bulbous smell norn-kind follow for bored 1.5 when night
grendel.bulbous smell ettin-kind follow for anger 1 when night
grendel.bulbous smell grendel-lair bring 5 1.5 drop 0.5

# Boney grendels: about by day, home to the lair at night, in a pack; they go
# for the weak (the care scent of babies, the ill and the senile) and run
# from crowds of norns; thieves, they snatch food and run home with it;
# mothers stay near the eggs.
grendel.boney smell grendel-lair follow 1.5 when night and awake
grendel.boney smell boney-kind follow for lonely 2
grendel.boney smell norn-care follow for hunger 1.5 when day
grendel.boney smell carrion follow for hunger 1.5
grendel.boney smell norn-kind avoid for fear 1 when here>0.5
grendel.boney smell grendel-lair bring 6,25 1 drop 0.5 when day
grendel.boney:female@youth-old smell egg follow 1 when awake

# Grouchy grendels, the C1 grendels (owner, 2026-11-27): clean (no germs, so no grendel scent for
# norns to learn to fear), gluttons and thieves that take food off to eat
# alone, loners that keep out of crowds and from other grendels, and seek
# out ettins, the only company they keep.
grendel.grouchy smell grendel-lair bring 6,25 1 drop 0.5 when day
grendel.grouchy smell carrion follow for hunger 1.5
grendel.grouchy smell norn-kind avoid for crowded 1 when here>0.5
grendel.grouchy smell grendel-kind avoid for crowded 1
grendel.grouchy smell ettin-kind follow for lonely 1.5

# Ettins: hoard gadgets and toys in quiet spots, carry eggs left lying about
# to the incubator, carry food to where creatures are hungry (when not
# crowded), seek norns when lonely and leave them when crowded.
ettin smell ettin-hoard bring 9,13 1 drop 0.5
ettin smell incubator bring 5 1.5 drop 0.6
ettin smell hungry bring 6,25 1 drop 0.4 when 9<100
ettin smell norn-kind follow for lonely 1
ettin smell norn-kind avoid for crowded 1.5 when 9>80

# ---- Examples, off (remove the "# " to try one) --------------------------------
#
# Harm and help leave a scent on whoever did it. A grendel that hits a norn
# carries "danger" for a minute and norns keep away; a norn that kisses or
# tickles another carries "kindness" and lonely norns go to it.
# scent danger fade 0.05
# scent kindness fade 0.05
# norn mark harm danger 1 600
# norn smell danger avoid for fear
# norn smell danger chem 10 2           # 10 = Fear
# norn mark help kindness 0.6 600
# norn smell kindness follow for lonely when 8>40   # 8 = Loneliness
#
# Dose bands: drawn to alarm when it is faint (curious), fleeing it when
# strong; and getting used to it if it hangs about.
# scent alarm fade 0.15 spread 0.2 habituate 600
# norn emit alarm 1 when 1>120
# norn smell alarm follow 0.5 when here<0.2
# norn smell alarm avoid for fear 1.5 when here>0.2
#
# Alarm: a hurt norn gives off alarm where it is; others leave the area.
# scent alarm fade 0.15 spread 0.2
# norn emit alarm 1 when 1>120          # 1 = Pain
# norn smell alarm avoid for fear 1.5
#
# Ant-like trails: a creature carrying something lays a trail; others with
# empty hands follow it toward wherever the carrying started.

#
